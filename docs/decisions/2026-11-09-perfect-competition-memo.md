# Perfect Competition — planting recommendation

## The plan

The best plan outcome previously stated would be 10 tomato beds, 20 carrot beds and 30 mesclun beds. Past 10 tomato beds, each extra bed costs more in labor than it sells for, so we stop there. Carrot and mesclun beds still make money right up to the most the farm is allowed to plant, so we plant every bed we're allowed.

Season profit at this plan: $42,775 (`model.xlsx`, Summary sheet).

## The judgment call

What I'd buy into first if we had the ability too would be increase one more carrot bed which would yield as an additional profit of 353, with one more mesclun bed (+247) as the second choice, where we should not even move towards any direction of spare beds or any extra labor which would yield us no return.

## What would change my answer

From our analysis from the table earlier the most sensitive item we have out the variables would be the mesclun price. If the mesclun price fell about 20%, the best plan would drop from 30 to 21 mesclun beds.
